**Karsus’s Folly: The Spell That Ended an Age**

In the vast history of _Dungeons & Dragons_, few tales are as tragic—or as awe-inspiring—as that of Karsus, the greatest wizard of Netheril. His ambition gave birth to the only known 12th-level spell, _Karsus’s Avatar_, a feat of magic so powerful it reshaped the entire world—and destroyed the empire that created it.

### The Prodigy of [[Netheril]]

Karsus was a child prodigy. It’s said he cast his first cantrip at the age of two, and by twenty-two he had become the youngest [[archmage]] in [[Netheril]]’s history. Becoming an [[archmage]] required more than talent: each was expected to create their own floating enclave, a city held aloft by the legendary _[[mythallar]]_—a vast magical engine that powered and sustained it. Karsus not only succeeded, but filled his enclave with impossible architecture that defied gravity itself, attracting scholars and artisans from across the empire.

Yet, despite his brilliance, Karsus’s youth and arrogance alienated many of his peers. He ruled his enclave as overlord, enforcing laws that reflected [[Netheril]]’s obsession with magic and hierarchy. Crimes against arcanists were punished with terrifying creativity—imprisonment within amulets, essence-draining undead, and even eternal torment. In contrast, killing a non-spellcaster was often settled with a fine. Magic was the law, and mages were above all.

### The Fall of [[Netheril]]

[[Netheril]]’s golden age began to fracture with the arrival of the phaerimm—monstrous, magic-devouring creatures that drained both life and arcane energy. Their hunger threatened the empire’s very foundation: its floating cities and immortal leaders relied entirely on magic to survive.

As the phaerimm spread, the life-extending spells of [[Netheril]]’s [[archmage]] began to fail. Even Ioulaum, the empire’s founder and creator of the first floating city, was forced to abandon his humanity, becoming a lich to escape death. His disappearance shattered the people’s confidence, and unrest swept across the empire—particularly in Karsus’s enclave, where citizens demanded salvation.

### The Creation of _Karsus’s Avatar_

Under immense pressure, Karsus turned his genius toward a single goal: ending the phaerimm threat once and for all. For more than a decade he labored in secret, investing unimaginable sums—tens of millions of gold pieces—in the creation of a spell that would make him a god.

The components required for _[[Karsus’s Avatar]]_ were as extraordinary as the spell itself: the stone-filled gizzard of a gold dragon, part of the Tarasque’s pituitary gland, and the bile of a twelve-headed hydra, among countless other impossible materials. The details of the ritual were lost forever, hidden by the gods so no mortal could ever replicate it.

The spell’s purpose was simple and catastrophic—it would allow the caster to replace a god of their choosing, assuming that deity’s powers and dominion. Karsus chose [[Mystryl]], the goddess of magic herself, believing her immense power would enable him to annihilate the phaerimm.

### The Moment of Ascension

When Karsus completed the spell, he succeeded. For a fleeting moment, he became a god. His body swelled with divine energy, and his mind expanded beyond mortal comprehension. But he had made a fatal mistake.

By seizing Mistral’s power, he had stolen the essence that maintained the Weave—the delicate fabric that allowed all magic to exist. With Mistral stripped of her divinity, the Weave unraveled. Magic across the world surged out of control and then collapsed entirely. Floating cities plummeted from the sky, including Karsus’s own. His petrified body fell from the heavens as his people perished around him.

Realizing his folly too late, [[Mystryl]] sacrificed herself to restore balance. In her final act, she was reborn as [[Mystra]], the new goddess of magic, who rewove the Weave with new rules and limits—ensuring no mortal could ever again wield such devastating power. From that day forward, no spell above 9th level could be cast.

### The Legacy of Folly

Only three enclaves survived the fall of Netheril. The survivors scattered across the world, and the cataclysm became known as _Karsus’s Folly_—a story told to every scholar of magic as a warning against unchecked ambition.

But Karsus himself did not truly die. The gods rejected his soul, and he never reached the afterlife. Instead, he became something outside reality—a _vestige_. Neither living nor dead, neither god nor mortal, he exists as an echo of arrogance and power, a remnant that some warlocks can still contact. Those who make pacts with this fallen archwizard report visions of a colossal, blood-red boulder—Karsus’s own petrified form—whispering in a voice that makes the earth tremble.

His gift to them is power. His curse is eternal bleeding—a reminder of the price of hubris.
