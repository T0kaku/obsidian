#worldbuilding
Never ran this, but I had an idea for a setting (where everyone is a little bit silly/stupid - Discworld style) where in the primordial chaos two pantheons fought - the Freedom loving Aesir and the Order adoring Archons. The Aesir where stronger and winning the war, but then Quetzalcoatl, the god of Dragons, ruined everything with various misadventures:

- First he invented death to reduce the armies of the Archons. This was done by a willing sacrifice of the orc god - meaning the Orcs lost their divine protection and got auto-cursed by the powers of the other gods (That's why their all moody violent death seekers).
    
- To counter act the fact that now mortals could die, Quetzalcoatl invented something called the Counter Law - if something as powerful as death ever occurred again, then an equal force would pop up to balance it - unfortunately this created devils and demons as a balance to the Aesir and Archons.
    
- The Counter Law created birth, but since every mortal had no gender that had to be invented. The Archons invented the male/female dichotomy, but before they revealed that idea, Quetzalcoatl suggested the idea of chopping of people's heads and the blood spilling out would congeal into new individuals. So Quetzalcoatl chopped of Titan's (god of Giants) head, creating two new deities: Tucker, god of Kobolds, and Zeus, god of demi-gods. However, Titan remained alive and his body got stolen by the Archons who used it as the bedrock of a solid-material reality (the current material plane) and thus get ensure that they gained power and became the dominant pantheon.
    
- Quetzalcoatl then decided to turn Titan's still living head into a clock - so now Titan is both Titan, the god of Giants and Chronos, the god of Time.
    
- Quetzalcoatl decided to switch sides, and leave the Aesir to join the Archons. Obviously they wanted his power but not his bad ideas; so they tricked him into entering what amount to a magic blender (convincing him that it was an initiation ritual) and split Quetzalcoatl in two. The metal Quetzalcoatl joined the Archons, having great knowledge and power, but no drive or passion; while the coloured Quetzalcoatl remained with the Aesir, who drop a pile of treasure on him to distract him with all the shinies. That's why metalic dragons are good but don't do much and chromatic dragons are always trying to build their hoards - so when they die it'll be added to the hoard that keeps Quetzalcoatl from acting on any more 'bright ideas'.

I haven't added much else to the setting idea, but four other things of note I remembered:

- ratmen are from the literal sewer of reality - the pipe lines that take souls to hell and remove any undesirably concept from reality - this has strangely left them with the largest archive of divine secrets + Eldritch lore while giving a level of atheism/misotheism depending on the individual.
    
- trolls don't turn to stone in sunlight - they get stoned. They never leave their cave in the day in fear of being ambushed in this weakened state.
    
- Halflings have a magical sense for food, this is why they end up in adventuring parties and not living quiet lives at home.
    
- No one, can remember gnomes exist(not even most gods) - they where given this memory magic to allow them to operate as spies/first attempt at earth elementals in the Archon-Aersir conflict and then their divine masters forgot about them, replacing them with halflings and modern earth elementals. Gnomes are actually one the rise as a civilization but everyone forgets them so they'll take the world by surprise soon enough; and then be forgotten as they rule over the world.
