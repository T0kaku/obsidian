 **Evocation (damage, energy):** Forceful gestures, thrusts, or slashes.
 **Conjuration (summoning/teleporting):** Complex hand motions, sigils in the air.  
 **Abjuration (protection, warding):** Defensive stances, shielding gestures.
 **Transmutation (alteration):** Molding or shaping motions.
 **Divination (knowledge, detection):** Pointing, tracing symbols, scanning motions. **Enchantment/Charm:** Beckoning, delicate flicks, or hypnotic patterns.
 **Necromancy:** Grim, clawed gestures or pulling motions as if drawing energy.

- **Scale the complexity** to the spell’s level:
    
    - Cantrips: Simple gestures (flicks, taps, small wave).  
    - Low-level: Sweeps, circles, or finger patterns.
    - High-level: Full-body movements, weaving intricate sigils, or dramatic flourishes.
