# The Island of Isled

_Region: Far Northwest of Orlèan_

An island in the far northwest of **Orlèan**, **Isled** is a land at war with itself. The island has not existed long enough for the truly ancient dragons of the world to take notice, but it has already become one of the strangest and most dangerous places in the known world.

Because flying predators and creatures capable of felling trees are common, most people live high in the trees in small settlements. These societies are usually small, defensive, and mobile, rarely forming large permanent communities.

The island’s curse has left it with nothing but megafauna and mountains made of diamond and gold. The soil itself is filled with precious gems and ancient coins. These riches are so easily accessible that the locals consider them nearly worthless.

Yet beyond the island’s shores these same resources remain unimaginably valuable.

---

## Geography and Environment

Isled is a land of extremes.

### Golden Mountains

Entire mountain ranges are composed of **gold-veined stone and massive diamond deposits**. In some places, the ground literally sparkles with loose gemstones mixed into the dirt.

### Infertile Soil

The transformation of the land destroyed the island’s natural fertility.

- Crops cannot grow in most places.
    
- Soil is mixed with metal and crystal.
    
- Farming is nearly impossible.
    

### Megafauna

The island is dominated by enormous wildlife:

- Giant horned beasts
    
- Massive reptiles
    
- Apex predators drawn by the island’s magical energies
    

Even creatures from distant lands are mysteriously drawn here, making Isled a **magnet for monsters**.

### Living in the Trees

Because of the dangers on the ground, most people live in **tree settlements**:

- Platforms connected by rope bridges
    
- Watch posts to spot flying predators
    
- Small communities hidden in dense forest canopies
    

Large cities are extremely rare.

---

# History of the Curse

Long ago a powerful wizard created an artifact capable of granting **three wishes**.

### The First Wish

The wizard wished to transform the land into **gold, diamonds, and precious gems**.

The magic worked perfectly.

But it had a devastating side effect:

- The soil lost its ability to grow food.
    
- Rivers became mineral-heavy and toxic to crops.
    
- Agriculture collapsed almost instantly.
    

What had become the richest land in the world was suddenly **unable to sustain life**.

### The Second Wish

Instead of fixing the catastrophe, the wizard used the second wish to create **a tomb greater than any ever built**.

The tomb is said to contain:

- unimaginable wealth
    
- ancient magical constructs
    
- the artifact itself
    

The **third wish remains unused**.

### The Rumor

According to legends, the artifact still rests somewhere inside the tomb, waiting for the next person powerful enough to reach it.

Some believe the wish could:

- end the island’s curse
    
- restore fertile soil
    
- reshape the entire world
    

Others believe using it could unleash something far worse.

---

# Isolation and the Deadly Sea

Despite its unimaginable wealth, Isled has **not collapsed the world economy**.

The reason is simple: **reaching the island is incredibly difficult**.

### The Ocean Barrier

The surrounding sea is one of the most dangerous in the world:

- constant violent storms
    
- unpredictable currents
    
- massive sea creatures
    
- magical disturbances
    

Only the most skilled sailors can reach the island.

Even fewer return.

---

# Political Situation

The island is divided between **two major power groups**.

---

## Clan Hoset (West Coast)

Clan Hoset controls the western shores of Isled.

They are weaker than the eastern clans and cannot survive alone.

To survive they made a deal with the **Kingdom of Deigh**.

### Protectorate of Deigh

Hoset now receives:

- food imports
    
- ships
    
- soldiers
    
- tools and equipment
    

In exchange they must:

- mine and export gold and gems
    
- allow Deigh political control
    
- attack eastern clans when ordered
    

The west coast has become incredibly wealthy by mainland standards, but **Hoset has effectively lost its freedom**.

---

## The Eastern Clans

The eastern clans refused outside control.

Instead they adapted to the island itself.

### Hunter Culture

They survive by hunting the island’s megafauna and living in hidden tree settlements.

This lifestyle has made them extremely powerful.

Typical ability levels:

- **Hunter adult:** Level 15
    
- **Normal adult:** Level 10
    
- **Child:** Level 5
    

Most adults are hunters because survival requires it.

### Reputation

The eastern clans are known for:

- extreme physical strength
    
- resilience
    
- independence
    
- hatred of Hoset’s alliance with Deigh
    

They remain free but constantly under pressure from both **Hoset raids** and the island’s monsters.

---

# Settlements

Large settlements are rare.

### Walled City

The largest settlement on the island.

- heavily fortified
    
- located in one of the few defensible areas
    
- trade hub between clans and outsiders
    

### Tree Settlements

Most people live in small communities built high in trees.

These settlements are:

- difficult for ground predators to reach
    
- easier to abandon if attacked
    
- spread out to avoid attracting monsters
    

---

# What the Islanders Actually Value

Because gold and gems are everywhere, the island’s culture values **completely different things**.

### 1. Food

Food is the most valuable resource on the island.

- imported grain from Deigh
    
- preserved meat
    
- rare edible plants
    

Food is often worth **more than gemstones**.

---

### 2. Fertile Soil

Actual living soil is extremely rare.

Small patches capable of growing crops are guarded fiercely.

A sack of fertile soil can be more valuable than a chest of diamonds.

---

### 3. Wood

Because many creatures can easily destroy trees, large strong timber is valuable.

It is used for:

- defenses
    
- ships
    
- fortifications
    

---

### 4. Safe Water

Clean water sources are scarce due to mineral contamination.

Springs with drinkable water are strategic locations.

---

### 5. Skilled Hunters

The island respects strength and survival ability.

The most valued individuals are:

- monster hunters
    
- navigators capable of crossing the sea
    
- warriors able to protect settlements
    

---

### 6. Knowledge

Maps, survival knowledge, and monster lore are incredibly valuable.

Someone who knows:

- migration paths of megafauna
    
- safe sea routes
    
- hidden fertile areas
    

can be worth more than any treasure.

---

# Why the Island Is So Dangerous

Isled has become a **high-level region** for adventurers.

The magic that transformed the land also empowered its creatures.

- Monsters here grow stronger than anywhere else.
    
- Powerful beasts from other lands are drawn here.
    
- Only high-level parties survive long.
    

Many treasure hunters come seeking the tomb.

Most never return.
