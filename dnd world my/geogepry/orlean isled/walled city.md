## Why It Wouldn't Be Much Larger

Several limits prevent big cities on Isled:

**1. Food supply**

The city depends on:

- river fish
- monsters
- crocodiles
- hunted megafauna
- limited imported food
- plants grown on the mountain side
- hanging herb gardens
- gather water as it rains so water is valuable in adition to plants
