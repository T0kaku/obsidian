Quark, the Greatest City That Is Or Will Be.

A walled metroplis of winding alleys and ever reaching towers, at the center of which is a fortress citadel upon which the great dragon Naztheros resides. The city was founded, built, and inhabited solely by goblins, which the Dragon-Lord freed centuries ago and instructed the mysteries of magic.

The lands surrounding the city are under mighty illusions, maintained by the Dragon-Lord, that have kept it secret since its founding. Any who cross its borders and breach the illusions are made known to the Dragon-Lord, and the goblins heed him utterly.

The city is ruled properly by a council of nine Magisters, each weilding a staff of office. They the greatest of wielders of sorcery amongst their kind and manage all aspects of governance and law. The Dragon-Lord largely remaining in solitude the fortress tower, which serves as the chambers of the Magisters and other high offices.

These goblins are unlike their lesser kin. While just as clever and opportunistic, these goblins are civilized, some even educated. Knowledge is power in Quark.

The greatest secret of Quark is its location. None have ever stumbled across their borders to stumble back. But many goblins do leave the city, sailing away on huge trading and exploration ships. Most goblins of Quark are uneducated and few have the skills to find their way home after weeks or months of sailing winding routes through open sea that makes little sense unless not being followed was a matter of life or death. Of which it often is.

Such expeditions are always lead by the elite of Quarkian goblins - magic users. Their duty is to serve the interests of Quark... and to silence any subordinates if they speak too much of their great city. Whenever a ship departs or agent is sent into the wider world, the Magisters place powerful spells that hold their tongues and even kill to prevent the knowledge of the city's location hidden.

Attempts by various cities or nations to capture Quark vessels or interrogate individuals usually end in frustration and dead bodies. Quarkian mages have been witnessed burning down their own ships and slaughtering their crew when capture seemed imminent.

The goblins of Quark and Her colonies (though farflung and isolated they are, and always with names starting with Q), have a reputation of being ruthless and suicidal. Their assassins are a legendary deterrent to prying eyes, and even an excellent source of profit for the city. Their fleets wander the seas, taking inane backtracking routes and avoiding more conventional shipping lanes. Their wizards travel in groups, at times advising lords and city magistrates, or exploring the lands in search for... something, presumably. Stranded sailors, mercenaries, or adventerous types can be found anywhere a Quarkian ship has visited, most unable to find their way home even if they wanted to.
