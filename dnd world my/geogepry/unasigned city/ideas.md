A village where all the buildings, horses, and people are mimics.

There have been a lot of caravans going missing on the roads near town.

The towns mayor (secretly a mimic) gives them a wanted poster (the wanted poster is also a mimic) for local bandits. The Mayor wants the bandits returned alive if possible.

After the bandits, the Mayor (a mimic remember!) wants some goblins taken care of, then some orcs, then some evil sheriffs men investigating the town. Returned to the town alive, if possible, so they can be tried. Then the town priest of Pelor (a mimic) asks the players to go get a group of acolytes from the cities temple of Pelor and bring them to the towns small chapel (a huge mimic).

Then the Mayor wants the players to go "steal back" 20 heads of cattle from the local farmer.

Of course the Mimics are eating all these things. You can eventually have a handful of paladins show up to defeat the players who are feeding these mimics and stop their evil. The Mimics of course insist the paladins are blackguards and liars and require the players help to defeat them. If they can be taken alive, this is best. Of course.
