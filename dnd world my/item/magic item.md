### Guardian Emblem

Wondrous Item (holy symbol), uncommon (requires attunement by a cleric or paladin)

Another item on this list requiring attunement by a cleric or paladin, the [guardian emblem](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/2412282-guardian-emblem) leans heavily into the paladin’s potential to support and defend their team. This emblem functions as your holy symbol, and can be attached to a suit of armor or shield.

There was a time when only [adamantine armor](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5370-adamantine-armor) could negate a critical hit, but the guardian emblem allows its bearer to do so three times per day. When you are wielding or wearing the item bearing this emblem and you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you suffers a critical hit, you can use your reaction to expend 1 of the shield’s 3 charges to turn the critical hit into a normal hit instead. Sure, you can already drop a mountain of d8s with your smites, but nothing feels more powerful than negating a critical hit.

Affectionately named Viviana & Tatiana by their last known wielder, these arcane twin pistols are crafted from a strange ore mined from a fallen celestial body. Viviana's frame is interwoven with a vibrant variant of mithryl mined from the upper planes, giving it a slightly radiant appearance. Tatiana's frame, on the other hand, is interwoven with a fiendish ore that gives the impression that the weapon bleeds shadow-like mist when fired.

  

The peculiar properties of the ore , coupled with the enchantments added during their forging process, causes them to be continuously drawn to be within 5 ft. of each other. The magic is such that if separated by a distance greater than 30 ft. they no longer provide their magical energy making you lose the magic effects. These pistols do not require ammunition, for the rounds they fire are not physical but rather magical in nature. 

You gain a

  

- You gain a magical +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with these arcane twin pistols.
    
- The magical nature of the ammunition fired by these pistols allow you to ignore the loading property commonly associated with this type of ranged weapons.
    
- Damage dealt from these pistols is in the form of  2d6 necrotic or radiant damage 
    
- The pistols hold up to 5 charges for "special rounds", which can be used as described below.
    
- Special rounds fired from Viviana deal radiant damage and those fired from Tatiana deal necrotic damage instead of piercing damage.
    
- You regain all charges after a short or long rest.
    

###### Viviana's Burning Light

- Spending 1  charge and a bonus action, you aim at a point above the target's head and release a shot.
    
- Viviana briefly charges up with radiant energy and then channels it down from the point where you aimed the shot dealing 6d8 radiant damage
    

###### Tatiana's Cold Touch

- Spending 1 charge and a bonus action, you fire a charged shot from Tatiana.
    
- This shot channels the cold necrotic energy inherent to this pistol dealing 6d8 necrotic damage
    

Parting Present

- Once per day the pistols allow you to cast homebrew Hex on a target within range.
    
- If you slay the target while it has your homebrew Hex on it, the round triggers a large explosion from within the target.
    
- All creatures within a 30 ft. radius of the explosion take 6d10 force damage. 
    
- If creatures in the affected area succeed a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, they take half of that damage.

Edit (Psychic Sword Details)

Yoto, The Cursed Blade +2 Longsword with the "light" and "versatile" properties 1d8/1d10 slashing damage

The handle is engraved with 4 closed eyes up its length and there is a 5th larger closed eye engraved within the hilt. One eye opens every damage tier you advance with this weapon.

When you make an attack you can choose to give yourself advantage for one attack. If you do you must make a wisdom save DC 15 (+1 for every time you use this feature and dont take damage, resetting back to 15 when you take damage) If you fail the save you take 1d4+4 psychic damage.

100-200 Damage: When you take psychic damage your move speed increases by 10ft until the end of your next turn. Your dreams are haunted by the misdeeds of the swords previous users.

201-300 Damage: You gain dangersense. Your hit dice are reduced by 1/4 of your level. Your dreams now include images of you turning on your allies and loved ones.

301-400 Damage: Once per short rest as a bonus action you can sacrifice one hit die and enter a Battle Trance for 1 minute. While in your Battle Trance you have advantage on Dexterity Saves and Checks and add your proficiency modifier to damage with this weapon. if you do not deal damage with this weapon during your turn while in Battle Trance you take 2d4+8 Psychic Damage. You can end your Battle Trance early as a free action, ending your trance early causes you to suffer 2d4+8 Psychic Damage.
