 This double weapon functions as a standard meteor hammer, dealing 1d8 points of bludgeoning damage with each end on a successful hit (20/x2 critical). The weighted heads of the weapon are hollow and may be filled with oil; with 1d3 full rounds of preparation, the wielder may ignite the weapon. Once burning, each end of the meteor hammer deals 1d12 points of bludgeoning and fire damage, and its critical threat range improves to 17–20 with a ×3 multiplier. The flames continue for as long as the oil lasts, usually one minute, after which the weapon reverts to its normal form until refilled and relit.
