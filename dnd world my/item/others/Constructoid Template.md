**Constructoid Template**

**Constructoid Creature**
Constructoid creatures are usually found living in the fringes of society, hidden away in sewers and ruins. Constructoids vary in appearance but resemble their original form, and are often about 50% mechanical with exposed machinery covered in glowing neon runes.

**Creating A Constructoid Creature**
"Constructoid" is an acquired template that can be added to any corporeal [aberration](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Aberration), [animal](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Animal), [dragon](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Dragon), [fey](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Fey), [giant](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Giant), [humanoid](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Humanoid), [magical beast](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Magical_Beast), [monstrous humanoid](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Monstrous_Humanoid), [outsider](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Outsider), or [vermin](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Vermin) of a (referred to hereafter as the base creature).

A Constructoid creature uses all the base creature’s statistics and abilities except as noted here. Do not recalculate the creature’s Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saves, or skill points if its type changes.

**Size and Type**
Type changes to [Construct](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Construct), the creature gains the [Living Construct subtype](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Living_Construct_Subtype). Size in unchanged, but weight is increased by x4. The creature retains any other [subtypes](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Subtypes) it had.

**Armor Class**
Constructoids gain an armor bonus of +2 and an arcane spell failure chance of 15%.

**Attacks**
A Constructoid gains a slam attack as a primary natural weapon. If the base creature can use weapons, the Constructoid retains this ability. A Constructoid fighting without weapons uses a slam when making an attack action. When it has a weapon, it usually uses the weapon instead.

**Special Qualities**
A Constructoid creature retains all the special qualities of the base creature and also gains the following qualities.

**Bionic Upgrade**: Creatures who become constructoids gain two upgrades from the following list. Each upgrade is considered an [augment](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Body_Augments). Constructoids increase their maximum augments by +2.

1. **Adrenal Stimulator**: +3 Initiative, -2 AC
2. **Audio Box**: Record and Playback up to 30 seconds of voices, music, or sound
3. **Carbon Fiber Plating**: Light fortification (25% to avoid critical hits)
4. **Cat-eye Module**: gain low light vision
5. **Coagulator Unit**: Immune to bleed damage, every round the constructoid is below 0 HP it heals 1 point of damage
6. **Copter Pack**: Shoulder slot occupied by a propeller apparatus. Allows for a 10ft fly speed (poor), and a 10ft swim speed
7. *Cybernetic Eye*: +2 on search and spot checks, +1 on ranged attack rolls
8. **Expanded Memory** Banks: +1 inherent bonus to Intelligence
9. **Extendo-Arms**: +5 ft Reach
10. **Foot Blades**: Rocket powered skates allow for double distance on charges and run actions.
11. **Grapple Launcher**: Occupies the arms slot, built in grapple launcher with a max length of 100 ft.
12. **Headlight**: Occupies head slot, behaves like a bullseye lantern
13. **Lead Plating**: +5 max HP, +1 Radiation Resistance
14. **Pheromonal Oscillator**: +3 on [social skill checks](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Social_Skills)
15. **Rampancy Protocol**: +5 current sanity, all sanity damage reduced by 1
16. **Reinforced Legs**: Fall damage halved
17. **Reinforced Spine**: +1 inherent bonus to Strength
18. **Rotary Joints**: +1 inherent bonus to Dexterity
19. **Synthetic Endorphins**: When below half health but above 0, the constructoid gains damage reduction 2/-
20. **Telescopic Ears**: No penalty on listening to things at a distance if focused on

**Abilities**
+2 Str, +2 Dex, +4 Con, -2 Wis, -2 Cha.

Upon becoming a constructoid, the creature loses 1d4 points of [sanity](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Sanity).

**Feats**
Constructoids gain [Toughness](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Toughness_feat) and [Iron Will](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Will_feat) as bonus feats

**Environment**
Any.

**Challenge Rating**
HD 3 or less, as base creature +1; HD 4 to 7, as base creature +2; HD 8 or more, as base creature +3.

**Alignment**
Any.

**Level Adjustment**
Same as the base creature +2.
