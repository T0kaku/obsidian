A hand cannon is a portable handheld cannon. It fires cannonballs, but grapeshot can be used just as easily. A hand cannon may be fired one-handed at a -4 penalty to attack rolls, but doing so increases the Minimum Strength required by 1. This gun requires at least 17 Strength to wield (two-handed); failing to meet the minimum Strength incurs a -4 penalty to aim and increases the recoil penalties by 50% (min 1).
