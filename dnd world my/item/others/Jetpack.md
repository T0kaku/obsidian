Jetpack

This arcanotech contraption is composed of a sturdy set of brass and leather wings, connected by a series of valves to a canister of arcane fuel for propulsion.

As a move action (or part of a charge) you can activate this item to gain a 40 ft fly speed. You remain flying until the jetpack is either disabled (as a free action), destroyed, or runs out of fuel. If airborne while the pack is disabled, you fall normally. It can ascend at half speed and descend at double speed, and its maneuverability is average. A jetpack user is treated as having the [Hover feat](https://triplecrit.fandom.com/wiki/Hover_feat) while using the jetpack to fly. Using a jetpack otherwise requires only as much concentration as walking, so you can attack or cast spells normally. You cannot use the run action while using the jetpack. If used as a part of a charge, you gain an additional +1 to hit. The jetpack can carry up to 300 lbs.

Consumes arcane fuel per round active (1/100).
