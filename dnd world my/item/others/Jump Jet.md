Jump Jet

**Price (Level)**
750 gp (3rd)
**Weight**

**Item Slot**
Shoulder Slot

This arcanotech contraption is composed of a pair of arcane fuel canisters for propulsion.

As a swift action you can activate this item to propel yourself vertically 15 ft, horizontally 20 ft, or provide a +10 to a jump check. If you collide with a sturdy obstacle such as a wall or ceiling, you take damage as if you fell the distance propelled. If used to propel vertically, jump jets do not inherently negate fall damage. If used as a part of a charge, you gain an additional +1 to hit. The jump jet can propel up to 250 lbs.

Consumes arcane fuel per use (1/50).
