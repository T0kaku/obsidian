#item**
The Wand of Wonder is a unique magic item that can be used by any character which states its trigger word. The wand must be aimed at a creature or item (“Target”), then the trigger word spoken by the “Caster”. Roll randomly to determine which table to use, then randomly to determine what the wand does. 

  

If the Target does not match the description for the effect, the Target changes to the nearest acceptable object or creature to the original Target.

  

If the effect is specified as zone or area, the area is centered on the Target unless otherwise specified.

  

“Ally” always means an ally of the caster.

  

If no duration is stated, effect is permanent.

  

Any effect bestowed by the Rod of Wonder may be removed with a Break Enchantment spell unless otherwise noted.
