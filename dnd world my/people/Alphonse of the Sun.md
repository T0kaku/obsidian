Contents

Early Life

Once an orphan of the local Peloric church, Alphonse was a devout and strong follower in the teachings of Pelor. He glorified the sun, worked hard to help his fellow man, and constantly worked hard in service of the church. Come 9 years of age, Alphonse had managed to impress and gain the notice of a philanthropic nobleman. The noble in question, Lord Bumbleson, was also a devout follower of Pelor and believer of doing the right thing. Impressed by Alphonses strong moral compass and desire to impress, he adopted the child and trained him in the ways of knighting. Once fully trained and ready, Alphonse set off on a mission with his close friend Dawn, the cleric. They set off far and wide to proselytize and ended up campus crusaders at a far off wizard college.

Career

Once a local naive wizard college preacher, Alphonse met up with some friends at a bar and tasted the best alcohol ever with his friends. When they woke up, it was three days later and Alphonse had a map inscribed in his back. Goaded with promises of treasure and religious artifacts Alphonse and friends set out on their adventure. On their way, they were sidetracked onto a planar adventure and gained companionship from Denira the Faun and a necromancer trapped on the plane of Ysgard. Merv was there too, banging extraplanar creatures. Upon escaping from the planes and returning to the material, Alphonse set out to return home, and perhaps pursue the prophesied love of his life told to him by the food dragon Nob. He also ended up becoming more plant than man, his connection with nature growing so strong after travelling through different arboreal planes.

From here, Alphonse decided to deal with Lazeron at a later date, and for now help his people and pursue his love. He traveled the land dealing with horrors from beyond, and foolishly letting a sinister Knight cheat his way back into life, tempted by curiosity. Alphonse had slain the Knight with the help of his close friend dawn, and his trusty steed Radiance. During celebrations following the destruction, he finally managed to find his fated love, and after much struggle and some help from Mr.Rubano, he gained the courtship of Helen, and eventually her love. The years passed peacefully after that, Alphonse was satisfied with the extraplanar journey, dragon slaying, and monster culling of his earlier years and entered a pseudo retirement. The Dawnstar keep, his new holding, became a popular spot for devotees of Pelor, and was where he finally married Helen. Before fully retiring, he made one last return to the Dennyplane at the behest of his new wife, here he gained the help of an alternate reality Rubano. This newbano painted back into his Human form, which he returned back to the material plane in and spent the rest of his days with Helen, Radiance, and the friends he made on his journeys. Alphonse, while initially tempted by the treasures and secrets of Lazeron, found that the journey was worth more than the destination, and found peace in the life he made for himself.

However, in the coming years after retirement, the second god war broke out. Alphonse, his soul joined with his wife, were blessed and reincarnated into an everlasting tree, a reflection of what he was during his adventures. Around this a holy grove sprang, there even persist rumors of a unicorn and pegasi resembling the mounts Alphonse had in his journeys.
