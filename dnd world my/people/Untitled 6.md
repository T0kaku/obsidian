Early Life

Daddy was born on Drow Christmas, in the year 8928. His "father", Kroagnar Vlumpiere, was gifted a wafer that would transform whoever ate it into a drow (which he then fed to a spider). Daddy, previously a daddy long legs, adopted his name and immediately began feeling the call of Lolth.

Career

Daddy, after obtaining a shotgun, became very interested in putting it to use. He has done little more than provide guard detail, and is being trained in firearm usage by other members of the Discovery crew.
