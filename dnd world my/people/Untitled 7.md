Early Life

It all began for Klagmar, formerly Samuel, when he was a child. Born to a loving mother and father, Samuel was an only child that lived in a small home while his father did his best as a mechanic on the ships that passed by and his mother did her best at home to keep things together. All Samuel knew was the floating island of Grunden, and to him a hunk of floating earth was normal. Though uneventful and small for a floating island, Grunden was a waypoint between larger city islands and many travelers passed through on their way to bigger cities and lands. This is how Samuel first learned how the world came to be, Samuel learned about the War of the Gods. Everyone, be it from in town or out, loved to put their own spin on the tale, but the base of the story never changed. The Gods had a disagreement over power, and wordwide war began as a result, this simple tale horrified young Samuel. How could the Gods themselves be capable of such malicious destruction? Were we nothing to them, like insects ready to be stomped on by the uncaring foot of Pelor, Boccob or Gruumsh? Such thoughts began to plague Samuel, he became increasingly convinced that any day the world would get split apart all over again! Samuel turned to books to quell his paranoid thoughts and irrationality, becoming more and more reserved as he aged, and spent his hard earned savings from doing odd jobs around the island on any tome or book he could find. By adulthood, he amassed an impressive collection that clogged the arteries of his parents home, while his parents tried unsuccessfully to wean him off of his book addiction. Unsatisfied with the little knowledge he knew, and dragged down by his paranoia, he sought out understanding of the arcane and made plans to leave the island for the closest college in an attempt to satiate himself. Selling his amassed collection of knowledge, Samuel regretfully bid his parents goodbye, and began his quest for understanding. As it turned out, the closest wizard college was "The School of the Sixth Sense" which was known as a respected, if sometimes unappreciated college dedicated to divination. Fitting Samuel to a T, he used his leftover funds for tuition, and began the long grind to becoming a wizard.

Years passed, and the college did little to quell his obsession with the War of the Gods. Samuel soon found that it only cemented his intense paranoia, and some of the oldest tomes contained inklings of other past cataclysmic events similar to the War of the Gods. This led to a horrific revelation by Samuel, that the prime material, if not the planes themselves, were going through a constant cycle of life, death, and rebirth. Either through the actions of the gods, or these other mythic cataclysmic events, the world would end again and again. Surely though, some variable could be found that pushed the world to these events? Based on his collected knowledge, Samuel concluded that if the gods went to war over some imbalance of percieved power then surely it must be because of good and evil! The morality of the world, if tipped too far in the direction of good and evil, would bring about another unmaking at the gods hands. At some point, too many stolen purses or righteous justice tipped the scales and the gods wipe the slate to start over. Convinced of this realization, whether true or not, Samuel began working twicefold to graduate the college. His paranoia driving him to higher heights and greater achievement, he managed to pass his classes with average marks. While good news overall, the concentrated exposure to the arcane began to change something in Samuel, and over the years he become warped. First came the weight gain, Samuels body quickly became dumpier and slower. After, it was the loss of his auburn hair, which fell out in patches that he believed were from stress. Then, his skin became a sickly yellow, growing increasingly turgid and less elastic with time, until his carapice became readily apparent. His pair of arms slowly grew into pairs, and his hands were eventually just referred to as claws, and eating was done with your mandibles of course. These changes took their toll on Samuels mind, but he found solace in small things as he attempted to comfort himself about his body. The extra claws helped with notes, and multitasking had never been easy! Pranks became nonexistant, as Samuel always "felt" them coming, not to mention his compound eyes helped take in so much more! Of course, his attempts at trivializing his condition only helped so much, and by graduation day he no longer resembled the young man that arrived at the college. Samuels graduation was short, and felt almost bittersweet as he left behind what felt like a new home. On this day, Samuel took the wizard name of "Klagmar the Prepared" and left behind what was left of Samuel, fully embracing his wizardhood. He slowly began forming a new plan as he packed his few possessions. He would survive the next end of the world. The gods who stomped on mortals as if they were nothing more than mere insects would not take this one. Klagmar the Prepared would outlast it all and show just what an insect was capable of. 

Hopefully he could make it in time.

Career

Long time wizard, Klagmar sells his services in his free time to amass money for research projects, magic items, and to invest in his planned Cataclysm bunker. He is currently under the employ of both the Dwarf Yakuza and of the Rebel sect run by Red the Rebel. When his services are called upon Klagmars usual action plan is to overthink any situation and use no common sense or social sense to address whatever it is he is dealing with. This usually leads to his team going loud the second his intricately woven planning falls apart like the glued together abomination that it is.

Recently Klagmar and his friends have acted as security detail for Pal the Paladin and managed to successfully snuff out all security risks so far

Following acting as security detail for Pal, Klagmar learned that Red was abducted! In an effort to save a perceived friend, Klagmar began rallying all that is good and holy to free Red from her abyssal prison of unending torture. During these ventures, he came across portals leading to the space between planes, and found the 3 Blind Dragons. One of these forgotten future seers told Klagmar the true history of the world, about the Overdeity, and the Primordials. His worldview shattered, Klagmar is unsure how to proceed now that his entire perception of the world is broken

In about a weeks time, after asking Kiera for help in dealing with his cosmic depression, he underwent intense training at the temple of Istus as he studied divine lore to deepen his understanding of the nature of divinity, the gods, and life itself. In the the end, he came out changed and hopeful for the future, now a fully fledged archivist and man of renewed faith.

After a couple of months more worth of corporate espionage and trying to find and free Red the Rebel, Klagmar eventually was contacted by the departed spirit of D4NKW0LF about something important left forgotten where he died about the coming apocalypse. Klagmar tried a multitude of different ways to find D4NKW0LF's remains. He concluded that D4NKW0LF must have been on Hathors Convent since it was the closest thing to the prophecies he was seeing about the belly of a beast. There he met some hacking nerds who were also invested in D4NKW0LF the legend and his exploits. Improvising, Klagmar worked with them to conduct a seance and summon D4NKW0LF's spirit for more information. Eventually, they learned of the great skywhale garbage disposal and it all clicked for Klagmar. The nerds accompanied Klagmar, willing to risk their lives and their expertise in the name of D4NKW0LF's legacy. Together, they all set out for the whale.

The following week ensued with Klagmar and friends setting out near the border of where the whale was located and purchasing all the gear necessary for an acid filled trip into the belly of the beast. Inside they encountered many strange creatures and people who survived inside the gut. The trip was fraught with many dangers and Carl even died once, before a quick resurrection afterwards. Klagmar also offhandedly adopted a bundle of orphans he found inside the beast. After whisking these kids to safety (after numerous teleportation mishaps) they continued deeper into the beast. In the third stomach of this monster Klagmar finally found an inkling of D4NKW0LF's resting place, the Black Ship. In the nearest port city, Klagmar rallied a crew together by mostly begging and giving fancy magic items away until a rather capable captain said alright. From here, they set out towards the ship.

During the trip over, however, they were passed by a WizcorpTM ship heading directly for their location. Anxious and paranoid, Klagmar was prepared for the worst and was ready to fight WizcorpTM head on in a no holds barred shootout. Upon arrival, they noticed the ship was crashed. Careful as always, the team decided dealing with the wizboys would be more prudent and searched crashed warship. The ship reeked of death, and the acrid smell of acid did nothing to help. Inside, the ship was coated with the blood and entrails of the hardened soldiers of the ship. Whatever had swept through was apparently so deadly that it inspired fear in even these soulless mercenaries. After a sweep of the ship, Klagmar realized that the ships data crystals were wiped clean and ordered his team to run for the Black Ship.

Upon entry, Klagmar saw D4NKW0LF's body cold on the ground and the computers in the process of wiping. He and the remaining hackers that accompanied went straight for the terminals to halt the process. Klagmar summoned all the divine insight he could muster and managed a near perfect halt of the wipe. Ever paranoid though, he picked up on the faintest sound coming towards him as his fingers left the keys and shouted a dispel magic spell before a blade plunged through his back and he fell to the ground, his vision blurry, before finally fading to nothingness.

When Klagmar woke up once again, he quickly took stock of his surroundings and noticed that he was indeed alive. Once he realized this, and he was in Hathors Convent once again surrounding by high ranking priests, he concluded his team must have won out for him to be present. After savoring a moment of pride at the fact his team could manage without him, he begged for more magical favors from the convent before teleportation back to the transport inside the whale. After talking his way out of getting shot by the readily alert crew, he crawled back into the Black Ship and re-equipped himself from his old corpse and greeted Kroagnar, the sole survivor of the fight. Undisturbed by the deaths of his friends (they could easily be brought back, no worries!) Klagmar saw what D4NKW0LF left behind for him.

After reviewing the videos he felt pride, sorrow, and fear. Predominantly fear. Boccob was dead, assassinated by Cosmo, and this wasn't the only Black Ship. There was a whole armada of these things, each prepared to deliver a Mana Orb payload to each of the Gods. Worst yet, the date of destruction was the turn of the new year, 7 days away. Klagmar began panicking before hurriedly realizing now was the time for action, not fear. He and Kroagnar ran through the ship and took what they could, noticed the cracked Mana Orb, and then warned the crew of the transport that brought them about the end times and the bomb inside the ship before ordering them to take out the planar engine (Later dubbed the "infinity engine" by Klagmar)

In the following days, Klagmar informed his network of allies of the end to come. The responses were varied, Sorcerer Co ran for the hills, others sent their people to hide outs and bunkers while some stood to fight. The following days were chaos as Klagmar downloaded Jebbert onto Zee's body and had him ready the bunker using Klagmars Ledger and funds. As the clock began ticking, Klagmar brought all his closest allies to his bunker in the cloud. On the way, D4NKW0LF managed to discover that the current vice admiral of Reds Rebels, Roy, was in fact a traitor and had apparently sold out to WizcorpTM in exchange for brainwashing Scarlet to be his lover. Worst yet, the Super Soldier line of fighters were WizcorpTM made and could be controlled by the now empowered Roy on a whim. Klagmar felt his resolve weaken with this news, and felt ashamed at his inability to stop Roy without endangering everyone around him and getting them slaughtered by unhinged super soldiers.

The good news was, despite Roy being a sellout and traitor, was that he informed WizcorpTM and they didn't exactly like Cosmos scheme for the end of the world and were mobilizing to stop him. More interesting was that D4NKW0LF managed to contact another version of himself and gained some coordinates. After heavy precautions, Klagmar and crew descended to the marked location as they passed over it on their way to the bunker. Here, they met the D4NKW0LF of another realm, accompanied by a WazzleClone. They told Klagmar and friends about where Cosmo was hiding, and how an army was marching on his fortress in the Astral Plane. They offered to take the group there and join this grand fight, but Klagmar knew he had to get his own people out of this world before even thinking of doing such a thing.

After parting ways, and giving the other D4NKW0LF a kiss, Klagmar went back to the ship and mobilized the crew to set sail as fast as possible. As they travelled, they saw as the world began to tear at the seams. The Astral plane bleeding into different parts of the cosmology, the return of Dragons, the world began to grind to a halt as the end arrived. Upon arriving at his bunker, Klagmar began setting up the ritual chamber. The Infinity Engine would power the entire thing, and it would be enhanced with different aspects he hoped would be present in this new pocket plane he was creating with everyone. While prepping the ritual, Kroagnar suddenly came running out the bathroom. His beard was no longer silver and he was excitedly showing off some sort of finely made ring. Upon touching it to examine, Klagmar found himself pulled and fused with Kroagnar. Disoriented, he felt his new body crazily shouting to start the ritual while another mind in the body conversed with Klagmar about how he would help them with the ritual.

One by one the mages assembled inside the ritual chamber (A gymnasium cleared aside for the ritual by Jebbert) and began to convene. All of the 100 mages left something important to them near the Infinity Engine and stood in carefully selected positions. The Kroag+Klag fusion began the chant, and the island shook with the quaking hum of thousands chanting as one. Klagmar pulled from all his cosmological knowledge and with Kroagnars bodily strength and fortitude they drove the spell with conviction, knowing that if they failed they cherished might be doomed to utter oblivion. The spell gained traction and became a thing all its own, it tried pulling everyone's very essence into the ritual as the spell become more powerful than Klagmar could have predicted. One by one nearly half of the ritual goers collapsed to the ground. Klagmar felt his and Kroagnars soul being pulled as well, but Kroagnars mountainous Dwarven force of will kept them from being pulled in as well. They uttered the final words of the chant, and with that they felt themselves pulled into a new realm, something more than just a demiplane.

They pulled apart, and Klagmar regained his composure once again. Kroagnar excitedly danced, glad to be alive and shook the gymnasium with a grand bellow of "We did it! We DID IT!" This joy was overridden when the dwarf noticed along with Klagmar the bodies that had fallen to the ground. Klagmar took stock of the assembled mages, a little under half had given their very being for the safety of all. He saw Carls drained form, he saw his socially awkward student Splog as well whom he had meant not so long ago when he had been going on his own religious journey. He approached to close their eyes, and give them a burial in this new world but their bodies turned into wisps of light and energy before joining the very sky of their new world. Klagmar wanted to grieve, but he was tired and drained from the ritual. He visited the people of the bunker, and told them of the losses and of the new world they were now in. Klagmar wasn't sure WHAT to do anymore. For all his meticulous planning and knowledge of nearly every topic in the world he had finally arrived at a moment when he didn't know what to do.

As he mused and took in the new, growing, world he had helped create side by side with Kroagnar and the rest of his team (Sans Chip, but Chip kept the children safe and that was good enough) he felt content, proud, happy to have saved at least these good people. Not a moment after this reflection were Kroagnar and Klagmar struck by rays of holy bright light and felt themselves suffused with power. Klagmar was the creator of an entirely new reality, after all, and being the Creator made you a God, did it not?

Kroagnar and Klagmar were taken aback by this, by the power they now held in their world. Klagmar felt power magnitudes above any of the divine or arcane power he had wielded before. With sheer force of will he gave rough form to the new Hall of the Gods and he felt it needed more. Kroagnar, in honor and pride of his Dwarven people, carved the rough looking hall into something truly divine. As Klagmar watched over the new world he noticed Carl, alive and well, as he walked through the doors of the Great Hall. Overjoyed that his friend was not lost to him, Klagmar listened as Carl told him and Kroagnar of those who gave their lives to create this world. He spoke of how they floated in the stars just as he had and how they were confused and needed guidance, unable or unsure what to do with their newfound divinity.

So the team once again convened, and assembled all in the Hall of the Gods. Just before Klagmar could address the Gods of the New World, the doors opened and a lone figure walked through. Possibilities ran through his anxious mind. Cosmo? A primordial come to destroy us all? He felt these worries wash away though as he looked at a being unfathomable to mortal minds. The Overdiety casually addressed the Hall, "I see you've created a new world and you're Gods now, allow me to give you a few pointers."
