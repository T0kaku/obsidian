This person comes from a group of traveling artificers but was separated from the group after an incident. Their goal is to find the group and become a master artificer. They may act self-confident to hide their weaknesses and true feelings.

  

They prefer working with a party but don't give out information easily and can be cold towards others. This person is a fusion between a robot and biological being and is trying to become worthy of being an official member of the group. They have morals but believe that development should come first.

  

This person is weak to alcohol and experiences mood swings, becoming either very friendly or angry. They have a fear of being discovered as a changeling, which can cause them to run away. Additionally, they will rage if someone misuses, wastes potential, or destroys their magic items.
