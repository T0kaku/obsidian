Project Name:Lazaruss

Location: Abandon building

  

**Project Overview:**

The lazarus Project was conceived by the state in order to create a new breed of soldier—one that could surpass human limitations.

  

The project was conducted in secret, targeting the lowest ranking species as even most slave species this were considered to cruel for. 

  
  

Background:

For years, she was subjected to intense experimentation. Forced genetic modifications, neurochemical conditioning, and brutal physical training. Each day brought new torments, pushing her to the brink of death only to be brought back to life. 

  

As the years went on, Kayle developed extraordinary physical capabilities. However, the state’s scientists were particularly interested in something far more dangerous. the magical ability. But there was a catch more the power was used, the more it damaged her body and mind.

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  

The Escape:

During a routine power test, Kayle's body began to fail under the strain of the energy she was forced to harness. For the first time, she felt something break within her, not just physically but mentally. In the chaos, Kayle was able to escape releasing a massive magic burst that shattered the containment cells and killed several guards and scientists. Confusion and alarms filled the facility, and Kayle, barely conscious, seized the moment to escape.  

  

Wounded and exhausted, Kayle hidd, disappearing  and losing motor functiuon. for centuries she stayed there hiding listening but unable to move but a few years ago a fire broke out making Kayle regain motorfunktuon she continued to live in the abandoned facility for some time until the empire tried to take over the building and science. the wanted  her as she was the closest thing to a success that remained.

  
  

But something had changed. Kayle could still feel the remnants of power within her, but it no longer flowed the way it once did. The magic that had once coursed through their veins with ease now felt distant, elusive—a flickering flame they could barely reach. The energy they had unleashed during the escape had burned out something vital, something irreplaceable. No longer could they call upon the full force of their abilities; much of their magic was gone, perhaps forever.

  
  
  
  
  
  

Goals & Motivations:

  

1. Revenge: Kayle is haunted by the memories of the lazarus Project—the horrors inflicted upon her and the countless others who perished in the name of development. her primary motivation is revenge. she wants to destroy the gearharts, expose the truth behind the project, and make the imperium pay for what they did.

  

2. Survival: Kayle’s escape from the facility was only the beginning. The state considers her a dangerous rogue asset, and they’ve dispatched elite teams to hunt them down. 

  

4. Finding Allies: Kayle cannot take on the imperium alone. One of her goals is to find others who have who are sympathetic to her cause—rebels, dissidents. These allies will be crucial in forming a resistance capable of taking down the imperium.

  

### Personality Traits:

  

- **Driven:** Kayle has a relentless determination to see their goals through, especially when it comes to bringing down the state. she won’t let anything or anyone stand in her way.

- **Haunted:** Kayle is plagued by nightmares and flashbacks of her time in the facility. These psychological scars manifest as insomnia, paranoia, and occasional dissociative episodes.

  

### Current Situation:

Kayle has been on the run for six months. The state’s hunt for her has intensefied. Kayle is currently hiding out in some abandoned mine.
