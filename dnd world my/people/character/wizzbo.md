### 📜 The Totally True Tale of Wizzbo the Unwise

(as recounted by Wizzbo himself, probably while shirtless and yelling at a goose)

Wizzbo was born as Grulg Meatfist in the Bonecrag Mountains, into a proud tribe of barbarian warriors known for solving all problems with yelling and axe-throwing. From an early age, Grulg showed signs of being… different.

While his siblings were learning to bite bears and wrestle trolls, Grulg was talking to the fire, making “magic potions” out of mud and squirrel tears, and insisting his pet rock "Rokthur" was actually a powerful spirit guide.

One fateful day, Grulg hit his head on a spellbook that fell off a dead wizard he was looting. He couldn’t read any of it, but he felt the knowledge soak into his very bones (it didn’t). From that moment on, he renounced his barbarian name and declared himself:

“Wizzbo the Unwise, Grand Archsmasher of the Mystic Order of the Flaming Elbow!”

He fashioned a “wizard staff” by duct-taping three warhammers to a tree branch. He donned a tattered curtain as a robe. He believes the more you yell a spell, the more powerful it is.

He wanders the land now, performing “mystical rites” such as:

- Casting "Fireball" (lighting a chicken on fire and hurling it)  
      
    
- Using "Mage Armor" (strapping a mailbox to his chest)  
      
    
- Summoning his "familiar", Rokthur, and throwing it at enemies  
      
    
- Chanting his “magic words”: “SHAZ-GRARG-KABLAMMO!” while pile-driving goblins  
      
    

Most wizards scoff at him. Most barbarians avoid him. But Wizzbo doesn’t need their approval. He’s on a quest to recover the Seven Mystic Nugglets of Eternal Crunch, an artifact he saw in a dream after eating a glowing mushroom.

He is, by all accounts, extremely dangerous… but mostly to himself.

For a **Swarm of 10 Weasels**, the swarm **does not make attack rolls** in the normal sense. Instead, it deals **automatic damage** to any creature in its space.

  

However, if you want to calculate an **attack modifier** as if one of the individual weasels were attacking (say, for a special case or grapple/Attach), here’s how it works:

  

### Individual Weasel (from Monster Manual):

- **Attack (bite)**: +4 melee  

  - **Formula**: +0 base attack bonus (BAB) + 2 size bonus (Tiny) + 2 Dexterity (Weapon Finesse used instead of Strength)

  

### Swarm Case:

- Swarms **do not roll to attack**—they deal damage automatically.

- If a DM allows an individual weasel in the swarm to attempt an **Attach** or grapple, the modifier would be:

  

> **Attack modifier (bite)**: +4 (same as above)

  

But again, in normal swarm combat, they **do not roll to hit**.

## Summary

  

This custom **Swarm of 10 Weasels** applies the Creature Swarm template to the Tiny Animal weasel (HD ½ d8, AC 14, Attach special attack) to create a Fine Animal (Swarm) with a pooled Hit Dice of 5 d8 (20 hp), an Armor Class of 22 (10 + 8 size + 4 Dex), and an initiative modifier of +4 (Dex +4)  ([Weasel :: d20srd.org](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/weasel.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [Creature Swarm (CR +0)[3pp] - d20PFSRD](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/templates/creature-swarm-template/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). Occupying a 10-foot space, it gains the swarm automatic bite attack dealing 1 d6 damage (for swarms of 1–5 HD) and retains the Attach ability along with Distraction from the swarm subtype  ([How do Swarms interact with Damage Reduction?](https://rpg.stackexchange.com/questions/202815/how-do-swarms-interact-with-damage-reduction?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [Swarm :: d20srd.org](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/swarm.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com)). It keeps the weasel’s low-light vision and scent, and its ability scores adjust to Str 1, Dex 19, Con 10, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 5 after applying the Fine swarm modifiers (–2 Str, +4 Dex)  ([Creature Swarm (CR +0)[3pp] - d20PFSRD](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/templates/creature-swarm-template/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [Weasel - d20PFSRD](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/animals/weasel/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
