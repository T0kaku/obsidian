The three ancient dwarven tinkerers played a pivotal role in developing and advancing technology in the region, but as they grew old, they settled back into the mountain.

  

After a couple of centuries of absence, eight new dwarves, who were descendants of the ancient tinkerers, emerged from the dwarven mountain. They carried on the legacy of their ancestors and set up shop in the nearest city, using their skills to fix broken technology, create new inventions. Their intentions were to assist and contribute to the world through their technological expertise.

  

For the next 800 years, these dwarves traveled from village to village, town to town, forming a traveling party of shopkeepers and inventors. They brought their knowledge, inventions, and repairs to various communities, sharing their expertise and continuing to enhance the technology of the islands. Their journey allowed them to spread their influence and contribute to the development of technology in different regions.
