The **Feast of the Traveler** is a holiday observed by both changelings and non-changelings. It honors the [[The traveler|Giver of Gifts]] but focuses on giving kindness to strangers—one of whom might well be the Traveler—and on sharing and celebrating.

It's often a big street party where all participants bring and share food and drink, people relax, business is set aside for the day, and gifts are given with nothing expected in exchange. Recipients quote "Beware the gifts of the Traveler" and raise a toast to the [[Dark Six]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Six "Dark Six").(https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Feast_of_the_Traveler#cite_note-RoE-p50-1)
