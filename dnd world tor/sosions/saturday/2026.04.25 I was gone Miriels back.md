# 2026.25.04


We all went down the elevator in Valentinos casino after Nuvi, Anni and Sunny killed Valentino. It led to a cave hallway where torches lining the cavewalls lit up with cursed flames and we smell rotten flesh. Sirius got a weird feeling because of the flames. 

Sunny and Nuvi sneak into the room ahead and behind two pillars. They see a dead crusified wolffolk surrounded by cultists. Using a wooden totem in a burninf chalise the leader summons fire wolf spirits before they disappear again. Behind Nuvi, Sunny spots another fire wolf jumping out of the flames of a torch and warns her. Sirius sets Anchors arrow aflame before Anchor fires the crossbow at the leader. 
Battle ensues. More fire wolves are summoned, some are killed. At some point the leader opens a portal. 

Switch to Miriel in the ethereal plane. She sees the soul of the crusified wolffolk appear and knows it will alert the Abberation to that location before she even hears it screeching in the distance. The monster says a name Miriel cant quite make out, but figures is the name of her father as it also mentions the name of her home village. She turns to see a portal opening and jumps into it, appearing in front of the party. The cult leader gets mad, yells at Miriel for not being the thing he summoned and gets grabbed by a monstrus hand shooting out from the portal before his soul is absorbed into it. The Abberation steps out. Miriel rages, the power knocks out Sunny, while the rest of the party feel a slight headache but stay alert. Out from the flame of all the 10 torches in the room, appears more fire wolves that all immediately run towards the monster and disappear into it. Nuvi shouts to Miriel to grab the totem before running to Sunny and helping her up. Miriel grabs the totem and the party all start to run back towards the cave hallway. The Abberation shoots infront of them and escapes into the city.

Miriel tells the party its headed for her father and her village (in the Faywild) and that they have to get going. Sunny suggests they get a wagon so, Miriel specifically, can rest. Leaving the casino, they are greeted with the sight of ruins and a noticeable lack of people.

Sunny finds the closest abandoned wagon with a horse and somehow manages to stear it (even though its the second wagon she has ever sat on). (Anchor may have left behind a coin in case the owner comes back.) The rest of the party get in, minus Sirius who flies above it, and start heading for Miriels village. Some take a long rest while others take a short rest as they switch between who stears the wagon. Nuvi, Sunny and Anchor try but fail to help or cheer up Miriel, the barbarian flinching at every touch, she’s not used to it anymore.

The party continues on their way to Miriels village in the (barrowed) wagon.
